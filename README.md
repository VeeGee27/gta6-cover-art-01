Magic Hour Cover Studio v2 — turn your photos into multi-panel GTA-style cover art: 12 layouts (including slanted Key Art Slash, Slashed Comic and Cast Grid), poster-formula lettering in Flat, Chrome 3D or Outline styles, 6 sunset and neon grades, gold and neon poster frames, crop by dragging with per-panel zoom, a photo library, Surprise me, and extras (starburst seal, pill badge, stars + quote, tags, cast role tags, callouts and a movie billing block) — then export a PNG in 1080×1080, 1080×1350 or 1920×1080. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make GTA 6 Cover Art" bounty, $250 in USDC.

## What's in v2

- 12 layouts, 6 grades, 3 frames, 3 title styles — up from 9 layouts, 4 grades, 1 frame, 1 title style in v1.
- New poster craft: Chrome 3D striped titles, cast role tags on every panel, callouts with a leader line, and a movie billing block under the caption.
- New sample art: two more AI-made Vice City illustrations (a neon street and a skyline at dusk), so the demo covers are one coherent poster instead of a mixed collage.
- Per-panel crop: tap a panel in the preview, then zoom it with its own slider or re-centre it.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — everything runs offline from your own files; photos never leave your machine.

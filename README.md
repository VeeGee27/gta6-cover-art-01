Magic Hour Cover Studio — turn your photos into multi-panel GTA-style cover art: 12 layouts (including slanted Key Art Slash, Slashed Comic and Cast Grid), poster-formula lettering in Flat, Chrome 3D or Outline styles, 6 sunset and neon grades, gold and neon poster frames, crop by dragging with per-panel zoom, a sample-art library, Surprise me, and dressing (starburst seal, pill badge, stars + quote, tags, cast role tags, callouts and a movie billing block) — then export a PNG in 1080×1080, 1080×1350 or 1920×1080. ## What this rebuild is

A from-scratch rebuild of the v2 revision, written fresh in one recorded session: every source file, the font, the sample art and the screenshots were made inside that session, so the transcripts account for the whole entry. Same feature set as v2, same unified-poster look — the demo covers are four fresh AI-made Vice City illustrations, so a fresh cover is one coherent poster instead of a mixed collage.

- 12 layouts, 6 grades, 3 frames, 3 title styles.
- Poster craft: Chrome 3D striped titles, cast role tags on every panel, callouts with a leader line, and a movie billing block under the caption.
- Per-panel crop: tap a panel in the preview, then zoom it with its own slider or re-centre it.
- Sky glow behind the title on key-art layouts; film grain over the whole poster.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — everything runs offline from your own files; photos never leave your machine.
